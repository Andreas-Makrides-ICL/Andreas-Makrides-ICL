## Andreas Makrides

PhD researcher at **Imperial College London**, building AI and optimisation methods for electricity markets, trading and energy.

I turn data into decisions in electricity markets: forecasting prices, pricing risk, valuing storage and flexibility, and solving market-clearing problems.

### Selected research

- **Learning to Fix: Optimisation-Aware Machine Learning for Accelerated Unit Commitment** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39396))<br>
  1st place in the 2025 EPRI AI-ccelerating Unit Commitment competition. 20.8× average speed-up over Gurobi at a 0.48% mean optimality gap. Submitted to *IEEE Transactions on Power Systems*.
- **Ancillary Services in High-Renewable Power Systems: Comparing Market Design, Emerging Trends, and Future Challenges** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37214), [source archive](https://github.com/Andreas-Makrides-ICL/ancillary-services-source-archive))<br>
  Market design for ancillary services compared across seven power systems.
- **Quantifying the Impact of Missing Risk Markets on Investment in Decarbonized Power Systems with Long-Duration Energy Storage** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13514), [code](https://github.com/Andreas-Makrides-ICL/incomplete_markets_long_duration_energy_storage))<br>
  Equilibrium models of how missing risk markets reduce investment in long-duration storage. Submitted to *Sustainable Energy, Grids and Networks*.

All six papers, talks and BibTeX are on the [publications page](https://andreas-makrides-icl.github.io/publications.html).

### Currently building

**Momentum Research Framework** *(private repo, code available on request)*

A systematic momentum research framework in Python: time-series and cross-sectional momentum, volatility targeting, shrinkage-based portfolio optimisation, walk-forward testing and paper trading.

### Skills

- **Machine learning:** machine learning for optimisation (predicting which binary decisions to fix in unit commitment), random forests with rolling-window retraining
- **Forecasting and statistics:** time-series forecasting of electricity and imbalance prices, probabilistic forecasts, state estimation, statistical modelling
- **Optimisation:** convex and non-convex (MILP, MISOCP, bilinear), bilevel and equilibrium models (complementarity), optimisation under uncertainty (chance-constrained, CVaR risk-averse), decomposition methods (Dantzig–Wolfe, ADMM)
- **Energy markets:** day-ahead and balancing markets, ancillary services, unit commitment and market clearing, storage valuation
