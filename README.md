## Andreas Makrides

PhD researcher at **Imperial College London**, building AI and optimisation methods for electricity markets, trading and energy: forecasting prices, pricing risk, valuing storage and flexibility, and solving market-clearing problems more than 20× faster than commercial solvers.

[Website](https://andreas-makrides-icl.github.io/) | [Publications](https://andreas-makrides-icl.github.io/publications.html) | [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=wIsge80AAAAJ) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andreasmakrides/) | [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-1795-0729)

### Selected research

- **Learning to Fix: Optimisation-Aware Machine Learning for Accelerated Unit Commitment** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39396))
  1st place in EPRI's AI-ccelerating Unit Commitment competition. 20.8× average speed-up over Gurobi at a 0.48% mean optimality gap. Submitted to *IEEE Transactions on Power Systems*.
- **Ancillary Services in High-Renewable Power Systems: Comparing Market Design, Emerging Trends, and Future Challenges** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37214), [source archive](https://github.com/Andreas-Makrides-ICL/ancillary-services-source-archive))
- **Quantifying the Impact of Missing Risk Markets on Investment in Decarbonized Power Systems with Long-Duration Energy Storage** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13514), [code](https://github.com/Andreas-Makrides-ICL/incomplete_markets_long_duration_energy_storage))
